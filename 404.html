  • Calorie Strategy : Moderate deficit (~300–400 kcal below TDEE of ~2200 kcal for 80kg sedentary-to-lightly-active male). Training days ~1850–1950 kcal. Core day ~1390 kcal.
  • Protein Strategy : Target 150–160g/day without whey. Achieved via eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, soy chunks, curd, chickpeas. Tuesday is the hardest day to hit — add curd or extra eggs.
  • Progression : Add weight to compound lifts every 1–2 weeks when you can complete all sets at the top of the rep range. At 1 month of experience, linear progression will be rapid.
  • Sunday : Rest day. Aim for 1600–1700 kcal, 130g+ protein. Light walking acceptable.
